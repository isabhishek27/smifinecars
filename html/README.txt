Sirius Motorsports Inc. — Static Website
==========================================

Pure HTML5 / CSS3 / Vanilla JavaScript. No build tools, no dependencies.
Ready to upload to GoDaddy, Hostinger, cPanel or any Apache/PHP shared host.

How to deploy
-------------
1. Upload the entire folder contents to your web hosting `public_html/` (or equivalent) directory.
2. Ensure `index.html` is at the root.
3. Done — the site is live.

To preview locally, simply double-click `index.html`.

Pages
-----
- index.html       Home / landing
- about.html       About us
- inventory.html   Full vehicle inventory + enquiry form
- financing.html   Trillium Financial info + apply CTA
- contact.html     Contact info, hours, form, Google Map
- terms.html       Terms & Conditions
- privacy.html     Privacy Policy

Assets
------
- assets/css/style.css   Global stylesheet
- assets/js/main.js      Vanilla JS (sticky nav, mobile menu, filter, form)
- assets/images/         (Reserved — current build uses royalty-free Unsplash URLs)

Customising
-----------
- Vehicle data is defined inline at the bottom of `index.html` and `inventory.html`.
  Edit the VEHICLES array to add, remove or update cars.
- Replace Unsplash image URLs with your own vehicle photos by uploading to
  `assets/images/` and updating the `img:` values.
- Contact details appear in the header CTA, contact page and every footer.
