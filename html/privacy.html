Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy

Last updated: January 2025. Sirius Motorsports Inc. ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information you voluntarily provide through our website, contact forms, financing applications and in-person interactions, including your name, telephone number, email address, vehicle of interest and any message content you submit.

Contact Forms

Information submitted through enquiry, contact and financing forms is used solely to respond to your request, process your application with our partners, and follow up regarding vehicles or services you have shown interest in.

Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences and understand how visitors interact with our content. You can disable cookies through your browser settings; some site features may not function as intended if cookies are disabled.

Data Protection

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect personal information from unauthorized access, use, disclosure or alteration. No system, however, is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of information transmitted over the Internet.

Third-Party Services

We may share information with trusted third parties strictly to deliver a service you have requested — for example, sharing financing application details with Trillium Financial. We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

User Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you, and to withdraw consent for ongoing communications at any time. Requests may be sent to the contact information below.

Children's Privacy

This website is not intended for individuals under the age of 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
